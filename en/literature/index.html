Books

The per-lecture References sections cite the works below by author and short title, with printed pages. Several use conventions different from this course’s (the slot in which the character inner product is antilinear, the order of composition, D2nD_{2n}D2n​ for our DnD_nDn​, French Young diagrams); the lectures translate at the point of use (see the Notation Bible).
