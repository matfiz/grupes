About this course

The companion to Finite Groups and Their Representations — structure, characters and the symmetric group.

What is here

14 lectures, 73 definitions, 114 numbered results, 113 proofs, 74 worked examples, 11 figures.

The course moves from finite-group structure to representations and characters, then to the symmetric group and Schur–Weyl duality. Notes, numbered results, figures and student sheets are generated from the reviewed LaTeX sources.

English is the primary source; Lithuanian is the default website language. Each lecture has four self-checks, including numerical questions. Study links lead to the relevant section; opening them does not record mastery.

Questions and progress

The student sheets contain questions only. Hints, answers and worked solutions stay in the source repository. Self-checks offer optional hints and an explanation after checking an answer.

Progress, outcome ticks and presentation annotations are stored in this browser. The optional Matfiz profile and backup tools are shared with sibling courses; finite-groups learning records have their own keys. No sign-in or server synchronisation is involved.

Conventions

Permutations compose right to left. Actions and modules are left actions. The dihedral group D_n has order 2n. Character inner products are antilinear in the first slot.
