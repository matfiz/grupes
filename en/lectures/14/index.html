Schur–Weyl duality

What this lecture does

Lecture 13 found the characters of Sn\SG{n}Sn​ as Schur functions. This last lecture finds the same functions as characters of GL(V)\GL(V)GL(V). On V⊗nV^{\otimes n}V⊗n, Sn\SG{n}Sn​ permutes the tensor places and GL(V)\GL(V)GL(V) acts in every place at once; each spans the commutant of the other. So V⊗nV^{\otimes n}V⊗n splits into pieces Vλ⊗Sλ(V)\Vlam{\lambda}\otimes\Schur{\lambda}{V}Vλ⊗Sλ​(V).
