Lecture 28

Symmetric functions and the characteristic map

We encode a class function of Sn\SG{n}Sn​ as a symmetric polynomial. Assigning cycles to variables gives its monomial coefficients a representation-theoretic meaning. Lecture 30 will show that the characteristics of irreducible characters are Schur polynomials. The same encoding turns restriction to the subgroup fixing one point into a derivative.

Where we are

The modules Vλ\Vlam{\lambda}Vλ of Sn\SG{n}Sn​ are labelled by partitions (Theorem 26.7). To determine their characters we use the classes indexed by cycle type (Theorem 6.4). The character inner product is antilinear in the first slot, and ⟨χU,χV⟩G\inner{\chi_U}{\chi_V}_G⟨χU​,χV​⟩G​ counts intertwiners by dimension (Equation (13.1) and Theorem 13.8).

28.2 Young permutation modules

Assigning a cycle of length kkk to variable xix_ixi​ gives a factor xikx_i^kxik​. Summing these assignments will count fixed ordered blocks.

Summary

  • The pμp_\mupμ​ form a basis of Λn\Lam^nΛn for N≥nN\ge nN≥n. The triangular change of basis counts assignments of parts to variables (Lemma 28.5).
  • The characteristic preserves the character inner product. The xμx^\muxμ coefficient pairs the class function with the fixed-block character of Mμ\Mmu{\mu}Mμ (Proposition 28.8 and Theorem 28.12).
  • The trivial character gives hnh_nhn​. For n≥2n\ge2n≥2, restriction corresponds to ∂/∂p1\partial/\partial p_1∂/∂p1​. Both facts are used to study the Schur basis in Theorem 29.9.

Answer to the self-checks

1. The two assignments.

In p3p1p_3p_1p3​p1​, choosing different variables gives each xi3xjx_i^3x_jxi3​xj​ once. Choosing the same variable gives each xi4x_i^4xi4​ once. Thus p(3,1)=m(3,1)+m(4)p_{(3,1)}=m_{(3,1)}+m_{(4)}p(3,1)​=m(3,1)​+m(4)​, and the second term has smaller length.

2. The singleton positions.

The two singleton blocks must each consist of a fixed point. For a transposition there are two ways to order its fixed points. They leave its two-cycle as the first block. A double transposition has no fixed point and fixes no tuple. The class indicator has characteristic p(2,1,1)/4p_{(2,1,1)}/4p(2,1,1)​/4, since z(2,1,1)=4z_{(2,1,1)}=4z(2,1,1)​=4. Using the monomial coefficient rule of Theorem 28.12, the answer is 2/4=1/22/4=1/22/4=1/2.

3. The class with one fixed point.

The centraliser order is z(3,1)=3z_{(3,1)}=3z(3,1)​=3. Thus the characteristic is p3p1/3p_3p_1/3p3​p1​/3 and its squared norm is 3/9=1/33/9=1/33/9=1/3. Its derivative is p3/3p_3/3p3​/3, the characteristic of the indicator of three-cycles in S3\SG{3}S3​. The type (3,1)(3,1)(3,1) restricts in this sense because a three-cycle on the first three points fixes the fourth.
