Lecture 11

The Jucys–Murphy spectrum

Lecture 10 gave each irreducible representation of Sn\SG{n}Sn​ a Gelfand–Tsetlin basis, whose vectors are separated by their weights α=(a1,…,an)\alpha=(a_1,\dots,a_n)α=(a1​,…,an​). To constrain these weights, we study sis_isi​ on the span of vvv and sivs_ivsi​v using an invariant inner product, then use the Coxeter braid relation. Every weight satisfies three conditions defining a content vector. We show combinatorially that these are exactly the vectors (c1(T),…,cn(T))(c_1(T),\dots,c_n(T))(c1​(T),…,cn​(T)) of box contents of standard Young tableaux TTT. Lecture 12 proves that every content vector is a weight.
