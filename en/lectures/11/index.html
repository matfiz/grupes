The Jucys–Murphy spectrum

What this lecture does

Lecture 10 gave each irreducible representation of Sn\SG{n}Sn​ a Gelfand–Tsetlin basis, whose vectors are separated by their weights α=(a1,…,an)\alpha=(a_1,\dots,a_n)α=(a1​,…,an​). Which weights occur? Every weight is a content vector, cut out by three conditions: the proof studies sis_isi​ on the span of vvv and sivs_ivsi​v, with an invariant inner product, and uses one Coxeter relation. Content vectors are the contents of standard Young tableaux. Lecture 12 proves that every content vector is a weight.
