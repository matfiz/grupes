The Gelfand–Tsetlin basis of SnS_nSn​

Block III opens with the representations of Sn\SG{n}Sn​. Can each irreducible be given a canonical basis, and which commuting elements of C[Sn]\grpalg{\SG{n}}C[Sn​] diagonalise it? Restriction along S1⊂⋯⊂Sn\SG{1}\subset\dots\subset\SG{n}S1​⊂⋯⊂Sn​ is multiplicity-free, so every irreducible splits canonically into lines: its Gelfand–Tsetlin basis. The Jucys–Murphy elements X1,…,Xn\JM{1},\dots,\JM{n}X1​,…,Xn​ commute and generate the algebra of elements acting diagonally in the GZ bases of all irreducibles simultaneously. Their joint eigenvalues distinguish the lines. Lectures 11 and 12 find which occur.
