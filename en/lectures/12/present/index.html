Lecture 12

Partitions, branching and Young’s orthogonal form

Lecture 11 proved that every weight of Sn\SG{n}Sn​ is a content vector, the content vector of a standard Young tableau. This lecture proves the converse by counting: admissible transpositions link exactly the tableaux of one shape, and Lectures 2 and 7 give p(n)p(n)p(n) irreducibles. So partitions label the irreducibles, tableaux label their Gelfand–Tsetlin bases, and restriction removes one box. A coherent choice of phases then gives the matrices of the sis_isi​: Young’s orthogonal form.
