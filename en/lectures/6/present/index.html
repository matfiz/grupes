6.1 Characters and class functions

Throughout, GGG is finite, and representations are complex and finite-dimensional.

6.4 Lifting from quotients

Theorem 6.14 (Lifting). Let N⊴GN\nsub GN⊴G, with quotient map q ⁣:G→G/Nq\colon G\to G/Nq:G→G/N.

  1. If ρˉ\bar\rhoρˉ​ is a representation of G/NG/NG/N on UUU, then ρ=ρˉ∘q\rho=\bar\rho\circ qρ=ρˉ​∘q is a representation of GGG on UUU with N≤ker⁡ρN\le\ker\rhoN≤kerρ, and χρ(g)=χρˉ(gN)\chi_\rho(g)=\chi_{\bar\rho}(gN)χρ​(g)=χρˉ​​(gN).   2. ρˉ∘q\bar\rho\circ qρˉ​∘q is irreducible if and only if ρˉ\bar\rhoρˉ​ is, and ρˉ∘q≅ρˉ′∘q\bar\rho\circ q\cong\bar\rho'\circ qρˉ​∘q≅ρˉ​′∘q if and only if ρˉ≅ρˉ′\bar\rho\cong\bar\rho'ρˉ​≅ρˉ​′.   3. The linear characters of GGG are the lifts of the linear characters of G/G′G/G'G/G′.

Proof. (i) ρ\rhoρ is a composite of homomorphisms, trivial on N=ker⁡qN=\ker qN=kerq, and ρ(g)=ρˉ(gN)\rho(g)=\bar\rho(gN)ρ(g)=ρˉ​(gN). Conversely, if N≤ker⁡ρN\le\ker\rhoN≤kerρ, then ρ ⁣:G→GL(U)\rho\colon G\to\GL(U)ρ:G→GL(U) factors through G/NG/NG/N in exactly one way, by Theorem 2.3(i).

(ii) As qqq is onto, the operators ρ(g)\rho(g)ρ(g) are exactly the operators ρˉ(gN)\bar\rho(gN)ρˉ​(gN). So the two have the same subrepresentations, and a linear map intertwines the lifts exactly when it intertwines ρˉ\bar\rhoρˉ​ and ρˉ′\bar\rho'ρˉ​′.
