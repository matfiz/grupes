What this lecture does

6.1 Characters and class functions

Throughout, GGG is finite, and representations are complex and finite-dimensional.

Definition 6.1 (Characters). The character of a representation (ρ,V)(\rho,V)(ρ,V) is the function χV ⁣:G→C,χV(g)=tr⁡ρ(g).\chi_V\colon G\to\C,\qquad \chi_V(g)=\tr\rho(g).χV​:G→C,χV​(g)=trρ(g). Its degree is χV(e)=dim⁡V\chi_V(e)=\dim VχV​(e)=dimV. It is irreducible if VVV is, and linear if dim⁡V=1\dim V=1dimV=1; then it is the homomorphism ρ ⁣:G→C×\rho\colon G\to\C^\timesρ:G→C× itself.

The trivial representation has the constant character 1G1_G1G​.

Proposition 6.2 (First properties). Let VVV and WWW be representations of GGG, and g,h∈Gg,h\in Gg,h∈G.

  1. χV(hgh−1)=χV(g)\chi_V(hgh^{-1})=\chi_V(g)χV​(hgh−1)=χV​(g).

  2. χV(g−1)=χV(g)‾\chi_V(g^{-1})=\overline{\chi_V(g)}χV​(g−1)=χV​(g)​.

  3. If V≅WV\cong WV≅W, then χV=χW\chi_V=\chi_WχV​=χW​. On V⊕WV\oplus WV⊕W, with g⋅(v,w)=(g⋅v,g⋅w)g\cdot(v,w)=(g\cdot v,g\cdot w)g⋅(v,w)=(g⋅v,g⋅w), χV⊕W=χV+χW\chi_{V\oplus W}=\chi_V+\chi_WχV⊕W​=χV​+χW​.

(iii) Isomorphic representations differ by a change of basis (§5.1), which keeps traces. In a basis of VVV followed by one of WWW, the matrix of ggg on V⊕WV\oplus WV⊕W is block diagonal, with blocks DV(g)D_V(g)DV​(g) and DW(g)D_W(g)DW​(g). 

Definition 6.3 (Class functions). A class function is a function φ ⁣:G→C\varphi\colon G\to\Cφ:G→C that is constant on each conjugacy class. The class functions form a vector space CF⁡(G)\CF(G)CF(G), with the inner product ⟨φ,ψ⟩G=1∣G∣∑g∈Gφ(g)‾ ψ(g).\inner{\varphi}{\psi}_G=\frac{1}{\abs{G}}\sum_{g\in G}\overline{\varphi(g)}\,\psi(g).⟨φ,ψ⟩G​=∣G∣1​g∈G∑​φ(g)​ψ(g).

The form is antilinear in the first slot and linear in the second, and ⟨φ,φ⟩G>0\inner{\varphi}{\varphi}_G>0⟨φ,φ⟩G​>0 for φ≠0\varphi\ne0φ=0. 1 We call ⟨φ,φ⟩G\inner{\varphi}{\varphi}_G⟨φ,φ⟩G​ the squared norm of φ\varphiφ; “norm one” means squared norm 111. The EklE_{kl}Ekl​-coordinate of AEklBAE_{kl}BAEkl​B is its (k,l)(k,l)(k,l) entry, (AEklB)kl=AkkBll,(AE_{kl}B)_{kl}=A_{kk}B_{ll},(AEkl​B)kl​=Akk​Bll​, and summing over kkk and lll, the map T↦ATBT\mapsto ATBT↦ATB has trace tr⁡A tr⁡B\tr A\,\tr BtrAtrB.

Theorem 6.7 (Characters count intertwiners). For representations UUU and VVV of GGG, dim⁡Hom⁡G(U,V)=⟨χU,χV⟩G.\dim\Hom_G(U,V)=\inner{\chi_U}{\chi_V}_G.dimHomG​(U,V)=⟨χU​,χV​⟩G​.

Proof. The dimension of the fixed subspace is the average of the traces of the representing operators (Proposition 5.8). Apply this to Hom⁡(U,V)\Hom(U,V)Hom(U,V) and use the character formula of Lemma 6.6: dim⁡Hom⁡G(U,V)=dim⁡Hom⁡(U,V)G=1∣G∣∑g∈GχHom⁡(U,V)(g)=1∣G∣∑g∈GχU(g)‾ χV(g)=⟨χU,χV⟩G.\begin{aligned} \dim\Hom_G(U,V)&=\dim\Hom(U,V)^G\\ &=\frac{1}{\abs{G}}\sum_{g\in G}\chi_{\Hom(U,V)}(g)\\ &=\frac{1}{\abs{G}}\sum_{g\in G}\overline{\chi_U(g)}\,\chi_V(g)\\ &=\inner{\chi_U}{\chi_V}_G.\qedhere \end{aligned}dimHomG​(U,V)​=dimHom(U,V)G=∣G∣1​g∈G∑​χHom(U,V)​(g)=∣G∣1​g∈G∑​χU​(g)​χV​(g)=⟨χU​,χV​⟩G​.​ 

In particular, ⟨χU,χV⟩G\inner{\chi_U}{\chi_V}_G⟨χU​,χV​⟩G​ is a non-negative integer.

Choose one representative of each isomorphism class of irreducible representations; write Irr⁡(G)\Irr(G)Irr(G) for this set.

  1. ⟨χU,χU′⟩G\inner{\chi_U}{\chi_{U'}}_G⟨χU​,χU′​⟩G​ is 111 if U=U′U=U'U=U′, and 000 otherwise.

  2. mU(V)=⟨χU,χV⟩Gm_U(V)=\inner{\chi_U}{\chi_V}_GmU​(V)=⟨χU​,χV​⟩G​, and χV=∑UmU(V) χU\chi_V=\sum_U m_U(V)\,\chi_UχV​=∑U​mU​(V)χU​, a finite sum over the UUU with mU(V)≠0m_U(V)\ne0mU​(V)=0.

  5. The irreducible characters are linearly independent in CF⁡(G)\CF(G)CF(G). So Irr⁡(G)\Irr(G)Irr(G) has at most as many elements as GGG has classes; and if kkk irreducible representations have pairwise distinct characters, and kkk is the number of classes, they are all of Irr⁡(G)\Irr(G)Irr(G), up to isomorphism.

Proof. (i) By Theorem 6.7 and Schur’s lemma (Theorem 5.13(ii)), dim⁡Hom⁡G(U,U′)\dim\Hom_G(U,U')dimHomG​(U,U′) is 111 or 000.

(ii) The first claim is Theorem 6.7 with Corollary 5.16. By Maschke’s theorem, V=U1⊕⋯⊕UkV=U_1\oplus\dots\oplus U_kV=U1​⊕⋯⊕Uk​ with each UjU_jUj​ irreducible, so χV=∑jχUj\chi_V=\sum_j\chi_{U_j}χV​=∑j​χUj​​ by Proposition 6.2(iii); and exactly mU(V)m_U(V)mU​(V) of the UjU_jUj​ are isomorphic to UUU.

(iii) One direction is Proposition 6.2(iii). Conversely, if χV=χW\chi_V=\chi_WχV​=χW​, the multiplicity formula gives the same multiplicities, and V≅WV\cong WV≅W by Corollary 5.16.

(iv) Expand both characters using the multiplicity formula, then orthonormality. The count. So there are at most dim⁡CF⁡(G)\dim\CF(G)dimCF(G) irreducible characters, hence at most that many irreducibles, since characters determine isomorphism classes.

6.4 Lifting from quotients

A representation of a quotient gives a representation of the original group by composition with the quotient map. We determine which ones arise this way.

Theorem 6.15 (Lifting). Let N⊴GN\nsub GN⊴G, with quotient map q ⁣:G→G/Nq\colon G\to G/Nq:G→G/N.

  1. If ρˉ\bar\rhoρˉ​ is a representation of G/NG/NG/N on UUU, then ρ=ρˉ∘q\rho=\bar\rho\circ qρ=ρˉ​∘q is a representation of GGG on UUU with N≤ker⁡ρN\le\ker\rhoN≤kerρ, and χρ(g)=χρˉ(gN)\chi_\rho(g)=\chi_{\bar\rho}(gN)χρ​(g)=χρˉ​​(gN). Every representation ρ\rhoρ of GGG with N≤ker⁡ρN\le\ker\rhoN≤kerρ is ρˉ∘q\bar\rho\circ qρˉ​∘q for exactly one ρˉ\bar\rhoρˉ​.

  2. ρˉ∘q\bar\rho\circ qρˉ​∘q is irreducible if and only if ρˉ\bar\rhoρˉ​ is, and ρˉ∘q≅ρˉ′∘q\bar\rho\circ q\cong\bar\rho'\circ qρˉ​∘q≅ρˉ​′∘q if and only if ρˉ≅ρˉ′\bar\rho\cong\bar\rho'ρˉ​≅ρˉ​′. So, up to isomorphism, lifting is a bijection from Irr⁡(G/N)\Irr(G/N)Irr(G/N) onto the irreducible representations of GGG with NNN in their kernel.

  3. The linear characters of GGG are the lifts of the linear characters of G/G′G/G'G/G′.

Proof. (i) ρ\rhoρ is a composite of homomorphisms, trivial on N=ker⁡qN=\ker qN=kerq, and ρ(g)=ρˉ(gN)\rho(g)=\bar\rho(gN)ρ(g)=ρˉ​(gN). Conversely, if N≤ker⁡ρN\le\ker\rhoN≤kerρ, then ρ ⁣:G→GL(U)\rho\colon G\to\GL(U)ρ:G→GL(U) factors through G/NG/NG/N in exactly one way, by Theorem 2.5(i).

(ii) As qqq is onto, the operators ρ(g)\rho(g)ρ(g) are exactly the operators ρˉ(gN)\bar\rho(gN)ρˉ​(gN). So the two have the same subrepresentations, and a linear map intertwines the lifts exactly when it intertwines ρˉ\bar\rhoρˉ​ and ρˉ′\bar\rho'ρˉ​′. Conversely, if an irreducible ρ\rhoρ has N≤ker⁡ρN\le\ker\rhoN≤kerρ, factorisation gives ρ=ρˉ∘q\rho=\bar\rho\circ qρ=ρˉ​∘q, and the same subrepresentations make ρˉ\bar\rhoρˉ​ irreducible. This proves the stated bijection.

(iii) A linear character is a homomorphism G→C×G\to\C^\timesG→C×, to an abelian group, so it factors through G/G′G/G'G/G′, by Proposition 4.13(iii). Conversely, a lift of a linear character is linear, by (i). 

6.5 The size of character values

The character value χ(g)\chi(g)χ(g) is a sum of eigenvalues on the unit circle. We determine when its modulus is as large as possible and what χ\chiχ tells us about the kernel.

Definition 6.16 (Kernel of a character). For a character χ\chiχ of GGG, its kernel is ker⁡χ={g∈G:χ(g)=χ(e)}.\ker\chi=\{g\in G:\chi(g)=\chi(e)\}.kerχ={g∈G:χ(g)=χ(e)}.
