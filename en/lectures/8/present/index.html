Lecture 8

Induction

Lecture 7 found every irreducible representation of GGG inside C[G]\grpalg{G}C[G]. Now a representation WWW of a subgroup HHH induces a representation of GGG, built from C[G]\grpalg{G}C[G] and WWW. Frobenius reciprocity relates maps out of an induced representation to maps between representations of the subgroup. Mackey’s formula restricts an induced representation to a second subgroup; together they decide when Ind⁡HGW\Ind{H}{G}WIndHG​W is irreducible. Inducing the trivial representation gives the permutation modules Mμ\Mmu{\mu}Mμ of Sn\SG{n}Sn​, whose characters Lecture 13 needs.
