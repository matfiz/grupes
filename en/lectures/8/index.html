Induction

What this lecture does

Lecture 7 found every irreducible representation of GGG inside C[G]\grpalg{G}C[G]. Now a representation WWW of a subgroup HHH induces one of GGG, Ind⁡HGW=C[G]⊗C[H]W\Ind{H}{G}W=\grpalg{G}\otimes_{\grpalg{H}}WIndHG​W=C[G]⊗C[H]​W. Frobenius reciprocity says that inducing is adjoint to restricting, and Mackey’s formula restricts an induced representation to a second subgroup; together they decide when Ind⁡HGW\Ind{H}{G}WIndHG​W is irreducible. Inducing the trivial representation gives the permutation modules Mμ\Mmu{\mu}Mμ of Sn\SG{n}Sn​, whose characters Lecture 13 needs.
