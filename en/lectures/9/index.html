Lecture 9

The arithmetic of characters

What this lecture does

Character values are sums of roots of unity, so they are algebraic integers. This lecture turns that arithmetic into theorems about groups: every irreducible complex representation has degree dividing ∣G∣\abs{G}∣G∣. Burnside’s theorem, where Block I meets Block II, says that groups of order paqbp^aq^bpaqb are solvable. Last, the Frobenius–Schur indicator decides which invariant bilinear form an irreducible representation carries.
