Lecture 3

Sylow theory

What this lecture does

By Lagrange, the order of a subgroup divides ∣G∣\abs{G}∣G∣; by Cauchy, every prime divisor of ∣G∣\abs{G}∣G∣ is the order of an element (Lecture 1). This lecture asks which divisors of ∣G∣\abs{G}∣G∣ are orders of subgroups. Every prime power divisor is: Sylow’s theorems give subgroups of the largest power of ppp, show they are conjugate, and count them. The count often forces a normal subgroup, and so decides that groups of many orders are not simple.

3.1 ppp-groups

The Sylow ppp-subgroups are as tightly controlled as they are large.
