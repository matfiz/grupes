Lecture 5

Direct and semidirect products

We describe a group by writing its elements as products of elements of two subgroups. The multiplication is componentwise if the factors commute with each other. Otherwise the second factor can change the first by conjugation.

Where we are

Every symmetry of a regular nnn-gon is rkr^krk or rksr^ksrks (Example 2.14). Conjugation by sss inverts rrr (Example 1.10). A normal subgroup is preserved by every conjugation, and kernels are normal (§4.1, after Definition 4.2).

5.1 Direct products

Take a turn of a triangle and, independently, a reversal of a segment. To compose two such moves, compose the turns on the triangle and the reversals on the segment. The two entries can be multiplied separately. This gives a group construction for any two groups.

Definition 5.1 (Direct product). The direct product G×HG\times HG×H of groups GGG and HHH consists of pairs (g,h)(g,h)(g,h) with g∈Gg\in Gg∈G and h∈Hh\in Hh∈H. Its multiplication is (g,h)(g′,h′)=(gg′,hh′).(g,h)(g',h')=(gg',hh').(g,h)(g′,h′)=(gg′,hh′).

For instance, let rrr be the triangle turn and ggg the segment reversal. Two repetitions give (r,g)(r,g)=(r2,g2)=(r2,e).(r,g)(r,g)=(r^2,g^2)=(r^2,e).(r,g)(r,g)=(r2,g2)=(r2,e). In general associativity holds in each coordinate. The identity is (e,e)(e,e)(e,e) and the inverse of (g,h)(g,h)(g,h) is (g−1,h−1)(g^{-1},h^{-1})(g−1,h−1). Each occurrence of eee denotes the identity in its own factor.

We recall three facts from elementary number theory. For positive integers mmm and nnn, their greatest common divisor gcd⁡(m,n)\gcd(m,n)gcd(m,n) and least common multiple lcm⁡(m,n)\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)lcm(m,n) satisfy gcd⁡(m,n)lcm⁡(m,n)=mn\gcd(m,n)\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)=mngcd(m,n)lcm(m,n)=mn. The integers are coprime when gcd⁡(m,n)=1\gcd(m,n)=1gcd(m,n)=1. By Euclid’s lemma, a prime dividing ababab divides aaa or bbb. Bézout’s identity gives integers xxx and yyy with mx+ny=gcd⁡(m,n)mx+ny=\gcd(m,n)mx+ny=gcd(m,n).

To find the order of a pair in a finite direct product, take powers in each coordinate. For a general product of subgroups the same insertion moves a factor from the first subgroup past one from the second. Normality of the first subgroup keeps the conjugated element inside it. A trivial intersection then lets us recover each factor from the product.

When these products exhaust GGG, we can describe the whole group by its two factors and the multiplication law.

Summary

  • Direct products multiply componentwise. The product of two finite cyclic groups is cyclic exactly when their orders are coprime (§5.1).
  • If N⊴GN\nsub GN⊴G and N∩H=1N\cap H=\trivN∩H=1 for H≤GH\le GH≤G, elements of NHNHNH have unique factors nhnhnh. Multiplication conjugates the next NNN-factor by hhh. If HHH is normal too, the factors commute (Theorem 5.3).
  • The pairing action gives S4/V4≅S3\SG{4}/\Kl\cong\SG{3}S4​/V4​≅S3​. The point stabiliser gives S4=V4⋊(S4)4\SG{4}=\Kl\sdp\Stab{(\SG{4})}{4}S4​=V4​⋊(S4​)4​ (§5.4).
