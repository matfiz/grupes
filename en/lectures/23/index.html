Part II — Representations and the Symmetric Group

Jucys–Murphy generation and the degenerate affine Hecke relations

What this lecture does

We use Olshanskii’s proposition to show that the Jucys–Murphy elements generate the Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra. Their joint eigenvalues then distinguish the lines of the Gelfand–Tsetlin basis. We relate these elements to adjacent transpositions. The local action in §24.1 leads to the classification of irreducibles by weights in Theorem 26.7.

23.2 Generation along the chain and separation

In §22.3, the formula Xk=τk−τk−1\JM{k}=\tau_k-\tau_{k-1}Xk​=τk​−τk−1​ puts the Xk\JM{k}Xk​ in the commutative algebra GZ⁡(n)\GZ{n}GZ(n), so they commute.
