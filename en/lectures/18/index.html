18.3 Mackey’s formula and the irreducibility test

Within a KKK-orbit, the induced space has a slot for each coset of the stabiliser KxK_xKx​ in KKK. The action on the slot at xHxHxH comes from the twist. This suggests describing that entire orbit by induction from KxK_xKx​ to KKK.

Summary

  • The multiplicity of a group irreducible in an induction equals the multiplicity of the input irreducible in its restriction (Theorem 18.2).
  • To restrict an induced space, take one induced summand per double coset. The input for that summand is twisted by x−1kxx^{-1}kxx−1kx on K∩xHx−1K\cap xHx^{-1}K∩xHx−1 (Theorem 18.5).
  • An irreducible input induces irreducibly exactly when it shares no constituents with the twists for double cosets other than HHH, on the corresponding intersections (Corollary 18.8).
